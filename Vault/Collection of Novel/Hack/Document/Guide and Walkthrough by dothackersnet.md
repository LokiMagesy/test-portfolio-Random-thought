Welcome to the Guide (And stuffs) for .hack//G.U. Volume 2 - Reminisce.

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: How do I get Wallpaper 21?!

A: Check the Salvador Aihara Quest Section.

Q: What's the maximum affection I can get in this volume?

A: Pink (No orbs outside)

Q: How do I change between Saku and Bo?

A: The appearance of Saku/Bo depends on Haseo's level. Even = Bo, Odd = Saku.

Q: For the Shadowless quest, how do I tell the wolfs apart?

A: Only one has a shadow

Q: What is the Terminal Disc?

A: The Terminal Disc is a DVD that explains the previous four .hack games, and
brings people up to speed on .hack. As you play along the three .hack//G.U.
games, more videos become unlocked, and you can watch more and more.
Unfortunately, the Terminal Disc was only included in the Pre-Order Special
Edition of .hack//G.U. Volume 1. You might be able to find it on E-Bay.

Q: Where can I get the Haseo Action Figure?

A: The Haseo action figure was also a Pre-Order Special Edition only
exclusive. Sorry. Don't worry, you're not missing out on much.

Q: Does anything special come with this game?

A: Yes. The first episode of .hack//ROOTS.

Q: What's the max level I can get?

A: 100.

Q: This guide rocks! Are you going to do one for Volume 3 when it comes out?

A: Yeppers. Based on the amount of E-Mails I get, I'd say people like these. :)

Q: Is there any way I can help?

A: Sure! Come join the dothackers forums and you can post information you
have that we might not. This is a community effort, after all, and everyone is
invited to help! =D

Most fields and dungeons contain one unlucky or lucky animal. You can
determine which animal it is based on the fact that lucky animals have a
musical note above their heads.

NOTE: This might be confusing to some, I'll try to explain it as best I can.

In this list, the animals are divided into 2 sections, the fields and dungeons. 
Within each section, there is a list of no.(1 to 0) at the top and the side.
The number on top is the value of the last digit of your average party's level.

--Meaning if i have a party of lvls 55, 56, 57, my average party lvl is 56.--

Hence the value is 6.

If you like, you can use Zefie's Average Level Tool to find the average level:

http://zefie.com/gu/

If there is any decimals when calculating the values, it's rounded down.

The number on the left is the last digit of the area's level.

Meaning if i enter an area of lvl 55, the value is 5.

The animal that appears will be chosen by the intersection of the values.

For instance, my average party level is 56, so the top value is 6.
The area I enter is a field level 55, so the side value is 5.
Comparing it with the table, I will get Leviathan in the field.

A quick note about Skeith
-------------------------
In the first few battles Skeith will have his form from the first game, meaning
a basic 3 hit combo when you attack.

After 1-2 battles, Skeith will have a form change, giving him new abilities
(Like 3 hit combos + 1 extra hit and a wider shot.) 

Attack Pattern
--------------
Basically, you just shoot like crazy at your opponent until he gets
paralyzed. When he does, just dash towards and hit him with your scythe; making
sure to dodge his attacks (All Avatar attacks are listed below)

After you've chopped away at his HP enough, you'll be able to data drain.
Basically, just hold O all the time to charge, and let go to shoot it.
(And make sure you hit, ok?)

6-7 silver looking shots. Most common attack and easy to dodge.

Basic shots.

ULTRA STRONG MELEE ATTACK OF DOOM!

Phase 9 - Lost Weapons (LWEAP)
------------------------

While playing the game (Sometime after you beat the Holy Palace), you will get
an email from an anonymous source (Phyllo from roots) that will give you a
lost ground keyword. In this lost ground, you will see 8 platforms, 7 weapons.
(The missing weapon is Ovan's) After some events, you will be sent to field,
in which a tutorial will pop up about the Adlers Keys.

The Adlers key's are random. In a field there could be 1-5 and 1-10 in a
dungeon. After getting some Adler Keys, go back to the lost ground and pay 5

The Fight with Doppelganger 
---------------------------

DualReact recommends the following strategy: Split the weapon usage, start with
a scythe. Use an air skill of the scythe and after that, either change to dual
blades or broad swords (dual swords on pirority). At about 1000 HP, use the new
awakening. That should do 1000-1400 damage which *should* kill him. Watch out
for his charm and paralyze.

Another strategy is to use Sleipnir, which will help you in next 5 battles.
You can suprise attack the Doppleganger, draining half his health.
Then, use a smoke screen. Then repeat. Eventually, his health will be low.
At that point, just fight him normally. If you use an awakening after those 5
suprise attacks, it will almost definitely kill him.

What is the "One Sin"?
---------------------

One Sin is the "undefeatable monster" that Balmung and Orca killed in
The World R:1 (.hack//AI Buster Volume 2).

In .hack//G.U. Volume 2, the One Sin returns, and it's pissed.


To get the One Sin event
----------------------

It's recommended to start this after you beat the game or before the last boss.
Use the forum posts :D
First, read the topic "Strange Event NPC". Then log in and back out.
Go to the forum and reply to a message in the thread "Ancient Egg".
Log in to The World, then out again.
Read the post reply and you will get keywords.
Go to the dungeon of the keywords you just got.
Oh look! It's those little annoying and dumb sorcerers from Volume 1!
Kill the summoned enemies, then go to the forum.
Reply to the new message in "Strange Voices"
Get the new keywords and go to them, fight, you know the drill.
At the end of the third time, you will fight the One Sin.
After that, you will get a mail with keywords to a lost ground.
There the REAL version of the One Sin - "The One God Eater" awaits you.

How to kill the One Sin 
----------------------
First off, the area level is 87, so being level 87 or higher is a must for this
battle to be any easier. Also, bring as many healing items as you need
(Depending on the level of your party members) 

Having a healer like Atoli,or a magic user like Sakubo, will make things a bit
easier.When the battle starts, you will see that the One Sin is a blueish
color, and only have the option of attacking its arms. Attack its right arm to
remove its protective barrier. Use any piercing attacks to get rid of it faster

Eventually, it will switch over to its left arm, and the One Sin will be a
purple-ish color. After doing this for a while, it's core will be exposed.

Attack it as many times as you can. Once you hear it roar and see it move it's
arms close to its core, RUN AWAY and call your party members to you (L1)
because he's charging up a big laser of DEATH. After the laser disappears,
attack the core. The barrier will come back up,and you will have to attack it's
arm again. During battle, the One Sin will attack you. One such attack is 
lifting up the arm that has a barrier on it, charging up a energy ball, and
slaming it into the ground. Another attack is taking the guarded arm, placing
it in the middle, making "circles" appear on the ground, along with a pointy
crystal. He will also just slam his arm into the center of the field. After
some time in the battle, he will also shoot the ground with a red laser. Stay
away from it, because a wall of fire will come from it.

What are the rewards?
-------------------

Once you defeat it, you will get the best accessory and armor in Volume 2.
You will also get a email saying congrats and keywords to the "God Eater" 

Both are level 100.

Tips:

which parts to input to the bike, as some parts will lower stability for higher
acceleration and vice-versa.

General tips: 
1. You will want to race in a map where you can see, right?
    Choose an area which is bright and is in daylight, instead of nighttime.
2. Learn the shortest path in the map by trying again and again. When you are
    in an open area and you are making a right/left turn, don't make a
    90 degree turn. Instead, turn early and make a curve turn. You want to save
    as much time as possible, right?
3. When you are stopped by obstacles/when nitro boost stops, do not restart the
     race immediately. Continue on to search for any possible places for you to
     save time in. Furthermore, you can train your skiing since there is no
     time limit.
4. Do not follow the steps blindly. If you find a route/approach that's more
    comfortable for you, use that route instead.

Tip submitted by Sotaka:
-------------------------------
Tons of people want to raise their characters to max affection for this volume.
Sometimes, this can be a lot harder than it sounds due to a lack of cards
And sometimes, people desire to conserve these cards.
Therefore, we have to find new, interesting ways of leveling their affection.

Therefore, the "Favorite action" will assist you in this task.
All of the characters have a certain "Favorite Action" aside from Rengeki's
that can help raise their affection faster. While trades and leveling with
them will also help with this, it might be faster to focus on their Favorite
Action 

There are three types of Favorite Actions: 

--Combo--
Combo Favorite Action is when you complete a 16-hit chain. The combo counter at
the upper left has to read 16 hits, and any beyond this will not affect it. For
example, you'll get credit for 16, but not for 32(Multiple of 16, so I figured
it would work - it didn't) or higher. Even after 50, you will not see it. I
also believe there is a limit of how m any times you can do this on a single
enemy (2 times, I believe), as it doesn't consistently work on bosses so that
you can just spam it. 

Characters: Sakubo (Bo), Anteres, Silibus, Pi 

Tips: It's recommended you go to an on-level area to accomplish raising fame in
this catagory. Take two Combo-users with you, so that you can get the bonuses
quicker for both of them and raise them at the same rate. 


--Killing blow--
Killing Blow favorite actions means just that : The character can get a killing
blow, and will gain the "Favorite Action" for it. 

Characters: Sakubo (Saku), Matsu, Piros the Third, Khun 

Tips: Killing blows can be harder to get. Some like to go to lower-level fields
and let their characters go all-out on them, which will let them gather up
killing blows fast. However, you lose the advantages of leveling / Rengeki's
when you do this. 

Therefore, my advice: Go to an on-level field, and set your party members to
Tenacity. With tenacity, they won't use Skills. Therefore, you can choose the
time when you want them to use their skills. Fight as normal, and get the enemy
down to 10% - The Doppleganger scythe is good for this, as it can reduce the
enemy HP count to 10% on it's own. After they get to 10%, have the character
use their strongest skill to get the kill. Do not have a Non-Killing blow user
there, as they will sometimes steal the kill from them, and then you won't get
the credit for it. 


--Critical HP healed--
This is healing a character who is in Flashing Red status. This is probably one
of the hardest to do, unless you're fighting in a severly overleveled area
(10-15 levels over you, depending on your own skill.) 

Characters: Alkaid, Atoli, Endrance 

Tips: Sometimes, it might just be faster to go with Rengeki / leveling with
these characters. However, some like to go to fields 15 levels over themselves,
and have the characters set on Tenacity so that they don't stunlock / kill the
enemies. Make sure haseo has an Item Doubler equipped, to reduce the risk of
DEATH O_O.

Phase 18 - Crimson Vs (CRIMZON)
-----------------------

What is Crimson VS?
-------------------------------

Crimson VS is a very simple game that actually plays by itself.
You create a deck which consists of one General card and three Unit cards.
Then you register the deck and the game handles the rest automatically.

Tutorial 
-------------------------------

When you first run the Crimson VS program from the Altimit Mine OS screen, a
short tutorial will appear. The tutorial walks you through the basics of
building a deck, testing that deck, and registering it online.
Once you have done all this, simply continue playing .hack as normal.

Every minute that the game is on, your deck will be paired up against another
deck and the two will battle. There is no way to stop Crimson VS from running
(even if the game is paused card battles will still happen). 

Tips 
----- 

Have Fun: 
Just as in real card games there is no such thing as a perfect deck. Every deck
is going to lose some of the time. With that in mind, don't be afraid to
experiment. Crimson VS can actually be a lot of fun despite it being a single
player game against AI opponents. Try building your own decks and see what
happens. Don't worry if you make a mistake. As long as you check in once in a
while, you will be fine.
(I made a deck that went 0-23! That didn't stop me from becoming the Champ!)

Watch Your Record: 
Every once in a while drop in on the Crimson VS and check your score
(your win to loss ratio).
If you find you are not doing as well as you would like, then think about
changing your deck around some. 

Watch The Logs: 
One of the handy things about Crimson VS is that it keeps a log of the last 30
card battles that happened. Check the logs if you want to know what kind of
decks your deck is losing against. (To view these, choose "VS" from the first
menu and then "Logs" from the second. Then scroll through and select the one
you want to watch.) 

Follow The Trends: 
Just like normal cards, Crimson VS goes through trends. That is to say, a
certain type of card will become very popular over other types. Look for this
when you are checking the Logs. If you happen to notice that most of the
players are running decks with all Assult cards (the blue hexagon with a wing
on it) then think about building a deck with Snipe cards (the red hexagon with
a gun on it) to give yourself the advantage. 

RTFM...or...Read the F***ing Message Board: 
As you progress through the game, be sure to check the Crimson VS message board
for tips on how best to play. A lot of the things discussed on the message
board are good tips and strategies for ANY card games, not just Crimson VS. But
if that's not enough incentive, there is also the three hidden cards... 

The Three Hidden Cards 
------------------------------- 

Flame of Consumption 
Restraint of Discipline 
Don't Kick!! 

These three cards cannot be acquired through booster packs. Instead they are
found in the chest inside the beast temple. But there is a special
requirement. If you have been checking the message boards as you should,
eventually someone will talk about the three hidden cards and remark that there
is a specific area word needed to find them. After a little more waiting,
someone will post the area word "Competing". Once you have this area word,
simply make an area with this as the first word and instead of an item in the
beast temple you will find a Crimson VS card. 

NOTE: You may get the same card more than once.
Simply revisit the area until you have at least one of each.
