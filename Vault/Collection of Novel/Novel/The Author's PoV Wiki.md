# Manuals and Skills of Ren Dover
_Note: Each realm signified a percentage of understanding. The minor realm of mastery required 25% understanding. The greater realm of mastery required 50% understanding, the essence realm required 75% understanding, and lastly the perfected realm, the pinnacle of art mastery, required 100% understanding._

- **[★★★★★ Keiki Style]** - Sword art created by [Grandmaster Toshimoto Keiki](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Toshimoto_Keiki "Toshimoto Keiki"). A five-star module that focused primarily on reaching the apex of swordsmanship and speed. Upon mastery, the sword art becomes so fast that before an opponent could even think about their next move, their heads would already be rolling on the ground.[[1]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-1)
    - Minor realm of mastery[[2]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-2) → Greater realm of mastery[[3]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:0-3) → Essence realm of mastery[[4]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:1-4)

- **[★★★ Ring Of Vindication]** - Highly advanced sword art that creates a perfect ring of defense around the user. Upon mastery, the ring can create a three-dimensional sphere that protects the user from all sides. Because of the lack of offensive capabilities, the manual was graded three-stars.[[5]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-5)
    - Minor realm of mastery[[6]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-6) → Greater realm of mastery[[7]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-7) → Essence realm of mastery[[4]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:1-4)

- **[★★★ Drifting Steps]** - Movement art that progresses with each step. With every step taken, user speed will increase. Unless user stops, speed will continuously increase until the user either runs out of mana or due to injury.[[8]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-8)
    - Minor realm of mastery → Greater realm of mastery[[3]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:0-3) → Essence realm of mastery[[9]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:3-9)

- **[★★★✰ Quick-Slash Style]** - A sword art fully focused on speed. With one quick draw, the user gains the ability to kill an opponent without them noticing anything had happened. Created to replicate the Keiki style. After years of research, this sword art was the result.[[10]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-10)
    - Minor realm of mastery[[11]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:2-11) → Greater realm of mastery[[9]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:3-9)

- **[★★★ Full-Body Martial Arts]** - Hand-to-Hand combat technique dedicated to utilizing every single part of the body to incapacitate the enemy. It may be a 3-star art, but upon mastery, it can be used to complement other forms of martial art.[[12]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-12)
    - Minor realm of mastery → Greater realm of mastery[[4]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:1-4)

## Skills[](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills?veaction=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Skills")

- **[{G} Monarch's Indifference]** - A skill that enables users to erase all emotions, and act as a supreme monarch that only calculates the best option regardless of circumstance.[[13]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-13)

- **[{D} The One]** - A skill that enables the user to instill fear into the opponents' minds resulting in them feeling like they were standing before a sovereign that overlooked everything. The skill may work on individuals who are one rank higher than them, however, if the difference between the two parties is greater than two ranks, the skill effect diminishes.[[14]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-14)

- **[{A} Eyes Of Chronos]** - Upon using this skill, the user will be able to see everything in slow motion. The more mana is used, the slower the time will be in their eyes.[[15]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-15)

- **[{?} Limit Breaker]** - ???????????????????????????????????????[[9]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-:3-9) Skill can only be used for five seconds. Cooldown period: three months.[[16]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-16)

- **[{A} Dark Servant]** - It will grant the user the ability to summon a shadow servant for an extended period of time. There will be 50% resemblance between the shadow servant and the user in terms of skill and strength. Cooldown period: 5 days.[[17]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-17)

- **[{S} Memory Manipulation] -** A skill that enables the user to alter the memories of the target or his own. It could only be used against those who were incapacitated or had their minds unguarded. The deeper and more memories changed the more mana is consumed. It can also allow the user to read others memory.[[18]](https://the-authors-pov.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_Dover/Manuals_and_Skills#cite_note-18)
