[Anarky](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Anarky) & [Anarky's Henchmen | Arkham Wiki](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Anarky%27s_Henchmen) 

Anarky's Henchmen were anarchists who worked for [Anarky](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Anarky). They were trying to remove corruption as they brought destruction.

To identify each other, they had red or white Anarky Tags printed on their clothes, wore white masks (or in rare cases, silver ones) and sometimes wore red wristbands with a black Anarky Tag on them.

Their main headquarters a restyled supply base at the [Solomon Wayne Courthouse](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon_Wayne_Courthouse) where Anarky was hiding.

Before Incident

Anarky recruited a number of [thugs](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Thugs) and criminals to serve as his followers and henchmen. Anarky set up up shop in the Solomon Wayne Courthouse and decorated it with his characteristic Anarky Tags alongside other graffiti. 

Incident

[Batman](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Batman) first ran across Anarky's Henchmen after he defeated [Deathstroke](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Deathstroke) and the [Electrocutioner](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Electrocutioner) on [Penguin's](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Penguin) ship, the [Final Offer](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Final_Offer). Exiting the ship, Batman was signaled by one of Anarky's Henchmen in a crow's nest. That first acolyte of Anarky didn't want to fight Batman just give a message to the Dark Knight from Anarky.

Anarky had placed three bombs at various locations within [Gotham City](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Gotham_City) to bring an end to the corruption and tasked his anarchists with guarding them. At each bomb location was a group of anarchists who opposed the Dark Knight. Once they were dealt with, Batman tracked down Anarky to his main supply base at the Courthouse, who along with his remaining henchmen, attacked him a final time.

- Anarky's Henchmen were a relatively small gang, who ranged in numbers from 50 to 100.
    
- Anarky's Henchmen were the fourth gang to use masks, the other three were [Black Mask's Henchmen](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Mask_Henchmen), [Joker's Henchmen](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Joker%27s_Henchmen), and [Bane's Henchmen](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Bane%27s_Henchmen).
    
- Anarky's Henchmen seemed to be the only gang that did not possess firearms.
    
- Several Anarky Tags can be seen painted on walls during the Knight incident. This could mean that Anarky still had some supporters about a decade later, or that they are painted by someone else who happened to like the symbol.
    

[BlackFire Cult](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Blackfire%27s_Cult)

Deacon Blackfire's Cult is a religious cult led by [Deacon Blackfire](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Deacon_Blackfire), a power-crazed priest who, by his own word, claimed to be an over 100-year-old shaman who declared himself immortal and begun brainwashing people to join his cause.

The cult is made up of the downtrodden, homeless and less fortunate elements of Gotham City whom Blackfire gave food and shelter while brainwashing into devout religious extremists.

Incident

The journalist Jack Ryder was investigating a mysterious cult leader named Blackfire, listening to his messages, examining the interrogations of his cultists, and reporting on the discovery of bodies. He continued with his investigation until he deduced Blackfire's whereabouts at the Lady of Gotham then went to investigate. When Ryder arrived there, he was captured by the cult and imprisoned, with Blackfire intending to use him as a human sacrifice.

Unbeknownst to Blackfire, however, [Batman](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Batman), suspecting that Ryder would get himself into trouble, followed Ryder's trail to the Lady of Gotham, arriving just as Blackfire and his followers were beginning the ritual. Blackfire, unfazed by Batman's arrival, ordered his cultists to attack and slay Batman as another "sacrifice".

Despite their superior numbers and fanaticism, the cultists were soundly defeated by Batman who then attacked Blackfire and knocked him unconscious, barely stopping him from murdering Ryder. Blackfire and the rest of his cult were all locked up at GCPD, where Blackfire would continue to preach.

Note: Unlike most other criminals found throughout Gotham City, who are little more than common criminals, thugs, and other amoral delinquents, Blackfire's cultists are strictly religious, and their objective is not power or personal gain but the blessing of God.

[Dollotrons](https://arkhamcity.fandom.com/wiki/Dollotrons)

Dollotrons were the final result of Lazlo Valentin "perfecting" a person, using identity-destroying chemicals and surgical treatments in a brutal process that left his victims irreparably scarred, physically and mentally. Dollotrons are victims lobotomized into essentially organic robots unable to think or feel anything. This makes them highly resistant to pain and only complete unconsciousness was enough to prevent them from mindlessly attacking their target. Doc, after he suffered a mental breakdown that made him utterly deranged, came to believe that people must have everything unique about them removed and/or destroyed if they were to become "better" and "perfect" as he saw it. 

As the ringleader of the Circus of Strange, Pyg kidnapped guests of all ages and both sexes during his shows where he used invasive surgery and personality-destroying chemicals to brutally mutilate his victims and "perfect" them into his Dollotrons. Those that he could not "fix" for one reason or another, were killed and thrown out like trash. 

Before Incident

Lazlo Valentin AKA Professor Pyg spent an unknown amount of years luring unsuspecting people into his Circus of Strange, so he could capture them and turn them into his Dollotrons, or murder them if he felt they were imperfect. His severe mental instability caused him to even break deals he made whenever he took hostages, such as when he turned a hostage into a Dollotron prior to the ransom being delivered, and proceeded to do the same to the man who delivered it. Pyg conducted the "perfection" process in an operating theater hidden underneath his Pretty Doll's Parlor beauty saloon.

Early History

Due to an unexplainable medical condition, combined with his exposure to an earlier variant of the [Devil's Breath](https://marvels-spider-man.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Breath) virus, a young Martin Li ended up receiving superhuman powers, inadvertently creating an explosion that killed his parents, and leading to his vendetta against now-Mayor [Norman Osborn](https://marvels-spider-man.fandom.com/wiki/Norman_Osborn), who was responsible for the accident. As an adult, Li spent years plotting his revenge on Osborn, leading to the creation of his "Inner Demons": several high-profile criminals from China whom he had corrupted into becoming his zealous enforcers. He then secretly had numerous numbers of them trafficked to Manhattan, New York via his shipping company. 

The Inner Demons first appear during an armed robbery of an art gallery with connections to Fisk, and take a hostage while searching for a file for the Devil's Breath. Authority is able to defeat the majority of the Demons conducting the robbery, although the remaining Demons escape with the file. 

During a political rally that Osborn, the Demons unleash suicide bombers to kill dozens of people. They are later seen following the terror attack arriving in cars to execute any survivors of the explosions. 

The Demons become a more active force afterward, spreading terror across the city, all while searching for Devil's Breath and trying to destroy Mayor Osborn's reputation. They clash with the NYPD, [Sable International](https://marvels-spider-man.fandom.com/wiki/Sable_International), and most of all, Spider-Man. 

After stealing the Devil's Breath from [Oscorp](https://marvels-spider-man.fandom.com/wiki/Oscorp), Mister Negative and the Inner Demons invade Grand Central Station with the intent to deploy it as a bioweapon. In the end, all of the associated Inner Demons are arrested along with Mister Negative, who is sent to the [Raft](https://marvels-spider-man.fandom.com/wiki/The_Raft) supermax prison.

After Li is defeated for the second time by Spider-Man, the Inner Demons are left to continue their criminal jobs without Mister Negative.

Characteristics

Based in Chinatown, the Inner Demons are mysterious enemies easily identified by their elaborate Chinese masks and more formal black-and-white attire. They operate under direction from Martin Li, aka Mister Negative. Some Inner Demons wield weapons infused with "negative energy" that can cause Spider-Man great damage. On occasion, Mister Negative will corrupt an Inner Demon or two through an extended direct touch, giving himself even greater power.

While a number of them are seen speaking English, the Inner Demons generally speak in Mandarin, suggesting that most of them are from China. Due to their corruption by Martin Li, the Demons share their malevolent personalities with their master through their dialogue and the orders they are willing to follow from their leader. These factors indicate that the Inner Demons are a fanatical cult-like organization rather than a criminal organization. This was also seen when Demon Warehouses were not fronts for illegal business dealings, but were in fact either terrorism staging grounds or explosives manufacturing facilities.

During the Osborn rally terror attack, the Demons are shown actively searching for survivors of the explosion to execute on sight, ignoring any attempts of the victims to plead for their lives. While fighting Spider-Man, their dialogue consists of an abundance of threats and boasting about the power given to them by their master. The Inner Demons have basic combat coordination, as they are heard giving direct orders to each other to make sure they can work as a team to defeat Spider-Man.

Enemies

The Inner Demons' units consist of all generic unit archetypes plus the Swordsman which is unique to their faction. The Demons' attacks and weapons are enhanced by Mister Negative's signature energy, allowing them to deal more damage than regular criminal mobs and rivaled only by those who are in possession of Sable tech. However, they are not much more resilient than Fisk's thugs. The faction's Brute unit might be the most dangerous of any faction's, as not only are they capable of standard Brute attacks, but also devastating shockwaves and AoE attacks that can take out a lot of Spider-Man's health in one hit.

The Inner Demons' arsenal typically consists of electrified versions of swords, whips, knuckles, and various other weaponry, including guns stolen from Fisk's weapons stashes.

Warehouse

The Inner Demons have possession of several warehouses throughout the city where they meet. Each warehouse will have several waves of Demons to defeat. The overall goal is to clear the area of enemies.
