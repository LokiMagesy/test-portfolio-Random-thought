# Where to find all the Lineage Types and Butterflies in Stranger of Sword City ([RPG BLOG](https://www.tehvidya.com/find-lineage-types-butterflies-stranger-sword-city/))

(**Update March 22, 2021**) _Stranger of Sword City Revisited_ was released in English recently, but this FAQ was based on the original Japanese release from 2014, so it might not be accurate. In the same way, my walkthrough of _Entaku no Seito_, now released as _Saviors of Sapphire Wings_, is based on the PSP version and not the updated remake, so it might not be accurate. **Follow this FAQ at your own risk!**

I finally finished my [barebones walkthrough of](https://www.tehvidya.com/basic-walkthrough-for-entaku-no-seito/) _[Entaku no Seito](https://www.tehvidya.com/basic-walkthrough-for-entaku-no-seito/), a.k.a. Saviors of Sapphire Wings,_ which made me feel all dungeon-crawly, which reminded me that _Stranger of Sword City_ came out in English recently on [the Vita](http://amzn.to/1T5biOh) and XBox One (**Update**: I’m replaying it myself now, this is a good chance to check whether the FAQs were right).

Since it’s in English and since it’s much shorter and more straightforward than _Entaku_ it doesn’t really need a FAQ, but I do remember having a hard time finding a few of the Lineage monsters in Sword City, so I thought I’d translate [this Japanese guide](http://www.spoiler.jp/srv/tsurugi/index.php?%E8%A1%80%E7%B5%B1%E7%A8%AE) to finding them all. I don’t know most of the English names of the monsters/levels, so I used my best guess in most cases. Please correct me if there are any mistakes.

Caution: Part of the fun of the game is hunting them all yourself, so only use this if you’re really stuck or frustrated. **Big thanks to Twinkle for fact-checking and corrections.**

### Butterfly Spots & Nests

_[![stranger of sword city fight butterfly](https://www.tehvidya.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/stranger-of-sword-city-fight-butterfly-300x169.jpg)](https://www.tehvidya.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/stranger-of-sword-city-fight-butterfly.jpg)Stranger of Sword City_ has both butterfly spots, where one butterfly shows up from time to time, and butterfly nests which are ambush points where butterflies respawn endlessly. The latter make good EXP grinding spots. I did my first playthrough on the Beginner difficulty so I never needed to grind, but I can see the use. Here are where to find all the spots and nests in the game:

They don’t always appear, and it takes a while for them to respawn once they do.
