attacking and building your moral, but don't use it until you can attack the
body.

easily be avoided. You may get hit by the wave, which should drain about 20? Only
drained 6 HP to me .. XD

your awakening ends he should have half hp, but be warned, he does like

down.

AIDA<Anna>

Attacks: White Shots
 Special: Single Beam

This battle should be a simple battle since his attacks are slow and you can

AIDA<GateKeeper>

Attacks: White Shots
 Special: Multi-Beams

This guy is really simple, he's just like the first 3 AIDA's you've fought, except
this guy has more White Shots that he sends out and does a Multi-Beam special attack
that can be easily dodged by hiding between the beams. Other than that, he's really
easy.

GORRE

Attack: Petal Shots
 Special: Painful Blow, Paradise Invite, Bloom of Destiny, Bubble Ship

She's really easy, her Petal Shots are easy to dodge, so is her Bloom of
Destiny attack. If you stay close to her for too long, she'll punch you. Once she
starts charging, prepare to dash left or right. When she uses the Bubble
Ship, hurry up and dash towards her and slash her, this is one
of her most powerful attacks and it drains quite a lot >_> If you get to low
on HP, she'll data drain you, make sure you mash your buttons as fast as
you can to minimize the damage.

AIDA<Oswald>

Attacks: Archene Shot, Kobold Bullet
 Special: Archene Web

Key to winning this battle is playing on the defensive stance.

This boss is very, very annoying... Ok when she uses Archene Shot, just slice
them away and keep shooting, the only time you should attack her directly is
when she uses Kobold Bullet, when she does this, hurry and dash in and do
a combo on her, this will slice the bullets away also.

She can also hit you pretty hard with just a melee attack so try and stay away
from that. The thing you have to worry about is the Archene Web special.. this
attack drains A LOT, I haven't found a way to dodge it quite good yet, most
of the time, I got lucky, but yeah, what I did was just dash left and kept
going. If you get caught it in, no big deal, she only did this attack to me
3 times before she died.

So basically, when she uses Archene Web, silce it away, when she gets ready to
use Kobold Bullet, go ahead and dash in and let a combo rip on her. When she
moves around, just keep shooting her and she'll go down eventually >_>

AIDA<Helen>

Attacks: Pascal Bullet, Prolog Laser, Photran Spin
 Special: Lips Tackle

This boss is so simple compared to Bordeaux, only thing you have to worry about
is the Lips Tackle which can drain a good bit of HP, other than that this boss
is easy. His Prolog Lasers can be easily dodged, when he uses Pascal Bullet,
just hit them when they're close. If you get to close to him, he'll use
Photran Spin which doesn't hurt that bad, other than that, this boss is cake!

INNIS

Attacks: 3-hit combo
 Special: Haze of Tresson, Confusing Flight

Ok this is one of those 'Epic' fights, Innis is really, really easy, becuase
most of the time she doesn't even fight and just goes on how 'No one looks at
me' etc. If she gets to close to you, she will use Haze of Treason which does
mild damage, nothing to worry about. Her special is Confusing Flight which can
be easily avoided, and when your close, just slash her to make her stop. Other
than that, this fight is simple.


CORBENIK

Attacks: Valiant Summon, Clince Needle
 Special: Devil's Verdict, Fearful Visitor

Corbenik is really simple, he has some devistation attacks if your caught in them,
but all of his attacks can easily be avoided. When he uses Valiant Summon, he
summons about 5 lessers AIDA<Anna> which can be destroyed with a single slash, so no
big deal on those. Clince Needles can be avoided by getting in between the Needles.
Devil's Verdict drains a lot if your caught in it but it can be easily avoided, and
same goes with Fearful Visitor, overrall, this fight is simple.

you've talked to him, log back out to check the [News] forum for the next hint.

Mac Anu by the way), once there, talk to him and he tells you to keep his little secret
quiet, after that, he gives you his Wallpaper and the Keyword: Nurse Carnival.

             steering in this point. Same theory, release the X button the

             turn. This requires some time and experience, it's not easy to
             avoid hitting the wall. Proceed from here to hit the 3rd k.C.C at

            to point 1 and move towards point 5 by turning right.

General tips:
1. You will want to race in a map where you can see, right?
    Choose an area which is bright and is in daylight, instead of nighttime.
2. Learn the shortest path in the map by trying again and again. When you are
    in an open area and you are making a right/left turn, don't make a
    90 degree turn. Instead, turn early and make a curve turn. You want to save
    as much time as possible, right?
3. When you are stopped by obstacles/when nitro boost stops, do not restart the
     race immediately. Continue on to search for any possible places for you to
     save time in. Furthermore, you can train your skiing since there is no
     time limit.
4. Do not follow the steps blindly. If you find a route/approach that's more
    comfortable for you, use that route instead.

  [] Crimson VS []
[]================[]

I'm not a big fan of Crimson VS so I'm not going to make a big section for it since
everything you need to know is in the booklet XD

Crimson VS is a card game played through auto-battle, Players create a deck of four
cards and register it for a battle. The deck will battle against other player's deck
automatically. You can ear more cards through battle in order to create stronger
decks.

Cards:
Here you can make and edit decks, as well as look at a list of your cards.

VS: Register for battle and view recent game results in the log.

Game Tutorial: View the Rules from Crimson VS.

End: Quit playing Crimson VS and return to the desktop.

Creating Decks:
A deck is made up of four cards, one of which is the General Card, and three
of which are Unit Cards. A deck is legal so long as the total cost value of
the Unit Cards is less than or equal to the General card's charisma value. You
can make up to 3 decks.

Battle Flow:

Battle starts off with the Unit Cards fighting against each other. The outcome of
the unit Cards' fight is determined by the cost comparison taking their 'trinity'
parameters into account. Unit Cards that win remain in play: cards that lose are
removed. Unit Cards are given Junction abilities, such as 'HP+1', and these
abilities are passed on from Unit Cards that win their fight to the General Card. When
the Unit Cards' three rounds of fighting have finished, a turn-based battle between
the General Card begins. The battle is won by the General Card that manages to
knock the other down to 0 HP, or the one that has more HP remaining after 10 turns
have passed.

Check the booklet on how the card looks XD I will be posting on the cards in the game
and the stats of them in the next update.
