-About- [XABO]
==============================

This FAQ is basically a transcription of all the Greeting Card e-mails in
.hack//G.U. Vol. 2. It lists all the your replies, the responses, and when
you get (or don't get) an increase in affection from your correspondent.

There are no 'dead end' replies. That is to say, no matter your reply in the
e-mail chains, you'll always have to choose from the next two, set responses.
Thus, with this format, I'll be giving both of Haseo's replies, and then their
correspondents' reply before moving onto the next two choices.

Basic example of this is like so:


***

[Saku: Re: I want to know you better]

+Affection Increase+

I don't have anything to tell you!
I might change my mind if you pay me though!



{R1}
[Haseo: Age]

I'm 17 and a sophomore in high school.
What about you?

[Saku: Re: Age]

+Affection Increase+

>I'm 17 and a sophomore in high school.
>What about you?

The same as Bo!

{R2}
[Haseo: School]

Do you like school?

[Saku: Re: School]

+Affection Increase+

>Do you like school?

I just make Bo do all my schoolwork for me!

***

As you can see, I also use addition ('+') signs along with text to point out
when you get an affection increase (If you don't, there will be no such text
added in the message). Or, in rare occasions, when you get a decrease in
affection, I'll use subtraction ('-') signs along with indicating text.

Angle brackets ('>') in the message are Haseo's previous text, as I did not
feel the need to erase it even though I typed in Haseo's previous message
anyway.

Square brackets ('[]') are the subject matter.

R1 means 'Reply one', and R2 means 'Reply two'.

I hope this does not cause any confusion.

Cards
---


In volume two of .hack//G.U., there are eight different possible greeting
cards to give out. They are:

'Poetry Gift'
'Hello!'
'Food'
'How are you?'
'Motto'
'Flower Gift'
'Hobbies'
'Challenge'

It should be noted that you can only receive the 'Flower Gift' card in volume
one, and thus can only use it if you had transferred your finished data from
the first game, and also hadn't already used it.

The Poetry Gift card is received from the first Ryu book once you reach the
maximum level (6) for volume two.

Most of the greeting cards purposes are self-explanatory, with the 'Food' card
asking what food the person likes, 'Hobbies' card what hobbies they have,
'Challenge' card that you want to defeat them (?!)...

The Poetry Gift, and Flower Gift's reactions vary, but it breaks down into
asking about the person in general, and simply giving them flowers.
