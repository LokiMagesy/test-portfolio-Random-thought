### **The Bone shark**
This evolution allows you to rend steel and hold on to the slipperiest prey.
Turns your tail into a skull-crushing bone sledgehammer.
Armors your head in an unbreakable bone helmet.
This evolution turns you into a spinning, bone-bladed blender of destruction.
When activated, this evolution gives you the Bone Crusher ability. It is recharged by biting or consuming creatures. Bone Crusher turns you into a bone battering ram, allowing you to shatter boats like glass.

### Night Shark/**The Shadow Shark** 
This evolution drains the blood of prey you bite, healing yourself in the process.
Allows you to launch a ball of poison from your tail when you tail whip. Creatures suffer -1% speed, damage resistance, damage output plus 0.2 damage per second for each counter, up to 30 counters. A counter is removed every 3 seconds.
Strengthens your neck muscles, allowing you to whip prey around with ease and lunge at great speed.
Releases a poison cloud around you when you evade.
Gives you the Shadow Form ability. It recharges when you bite or consume a creature. Shadow Form slows the world around you while also releasing an aura of poison each time you lunge.

Mutation by pollution or Cyborg;

### **The Bio-Electric Shark**
On bite you release an electrical shock, inflicting 1 damage and putting 1 stun counter on anything within 1.5m.
Turns your tail into a lightning projector. A target is stunned when it accumulates 10 stun counters. A stun counter is removed every 3 seconds.
This evolution releases an electric shock when you ram boats, short-circuiting them.
On evade you turn into lightning, inflicting 4 damage and putting 1 stun counter on anything within 3 meters.
When activated, this evolution gives you the Lightning Burst ability. It is recharged by damaging or consuming creatures. Lightning Burst cloaks you with a lightning aura, electrocuting anything that comes near.

Serum Drop (Medicinal Drug and illegal Drug dropping on a shark; similar to cocaine bear)
